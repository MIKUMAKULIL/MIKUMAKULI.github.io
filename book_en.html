About the Monograph "Friends We Didn't Know About"

This fundamental compendium stands as the definitive outcome of a twenty-year transcontinental empirical investigation, precisely aimed at the determination and verification of hidden, endemic, and critically endangered representatives of fauna on the verge of extinction due to increasing technogenic pressure. Professor Merlin Shakespeare applies his unique synergistic biocenotic method, which scientifically demonstrates that closed-type ecosystems contain stable populations of higher organisms hitherto completely ignored by official nomenclature biology.

Special emphasis in this peer-reviewed edition is placed on the mathematical modeling of species survival under conditions of severe radioecological and chemical stress. The volume is comprehensively equipped with unique migration mapping, biomass formulas, and personal verbatim transcripts of field interviews conducted with gamekeepers of national reserves across both the USSR and Western Europe.
